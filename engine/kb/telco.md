# Sample knowledge base: fictional Philippine fiber ISP

Each `## ` section is one procedure the engine can retrieve. Replace or extend for the demo.

## Slow internet or no connection
Ask which lights are on the modem. If the LOS light is red, the fiber line is down: do not power cycle, open a repair ticket and schedule a technician within 24-48 hours. If no red light: guide a power cycle (unplug 30 seconds), then run a speed test on fast.com while connected by LAN cable.

## Billing dispute or double charge
Verify the account with full name and registered mobile number. Pull the last 3 statements. If a double charge is confirmed, file a billing adjustment; it reflects on the next statement. A one-time courtesy credit of up to PHP 500 may be offered for service-affecting issues.

## Cancel subscription
Ask for the reason. Offer the retention promo: 50% off the monthly fee for 3 months. If the customer still wants to cancel, check the lock-in period; pre-termination fee applies if within 24 months. Modem must be returned within 30 days.

## Change billing address or account details
Verify identity with 2 security questions (birthday, last payment amount). Update the address in the system and confirm through SMS to the registered mobile number.

## Payment not reflected
Ask for the reference number, channel (GCash, Maya, bank, over-the-counter) and date. Payments via e-wallets post within 24 hours, over-the-counter within 3 banking days. If past that window, file a payment trace ticket and advise the customer not to pay again.

## Upgrade plan or speed
Check eligibility (no overdue balance). Available plans: 300 Mbps at PHP 1,299, 500 Mbps at PHP 1,699, 1 Gbps at PHP 2,499. Upgrade takes effect within 24 hours with no new lock-in if the account is past 12 months.

## Relocation or transfer of service
Check fiber availability at the new address. Relocation fee is PHP 1,000, waived for accounts in good standing past 12 months. Schedule installation 3-7 days out.

## Angry or escalating customer
Acknowledge the frustration first, apologize for the inconvenience, and summarize the issue back. Do not argue. If the customer asks for a supervisor, offer a callback within 24 hours and log the escalation.
